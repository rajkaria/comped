Get on the board. Find out where you land.

One line, about ten seconds, no account. It reads your AI tools' logs on your own computer, prints your card, and posts just the score. This one goes up as anon-xxxx:

$ curl -fsSL https://gotcomped.com/comped.sh | sh

Want your name on the row instead? Settings go on the end of the same line, after sh -s --. Copy this and change yourname:

$ curl -fsSL https://gotcomped.com/comped.sh | sh -s -- handle=yourname

The -s -- is what makes sh pass the setting through; | sh handle=yourname will not work. Settings stack, separated by spaces: … | sh -s -- handle=yourname plan=claude-pro-20, and leaderboard=false prints the card without posting anything. Handles are letters, digits, _, . and -, up to 32 characters; a re-run replaces your row rather than adding one. On node it is the same words: npx comped handle=yourname.
